# Cancellation policy

## Who can cancel
Only the customer who placed the order can ask for a cancellation. The customer must be verified with the order number and the email address used for the order.

## Cancellation window
An order can be cancelled within 24 hours of being placed, as long as it has not shipped yet. After 24 hours the order is already being prepared and can no longer be cancelled.

## After shipping
Once an order has shipped it cannot be cancelled. The customer can return it after delivery, see the refund policy.

## Refund on cancellation
A cancelled order is refunded in full to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.

## Approval
Every cancellation must be approved by a support specialist before it is completed.
