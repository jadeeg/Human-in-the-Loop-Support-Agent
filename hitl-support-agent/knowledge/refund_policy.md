# Refund policy

## Refund window
A refund can be requested for a delivered order within 30 days after the delivery date. After 30 days refunds are no longer possible.

## Refund amount
The refund covers the order amount paid. The refund goes back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days. An order can only be refunded once.

## Orders not yet delivered
Orders that have not been delivered cannot be refunded. If the order has not shipped, the customer can ask to cancel it instead.

## Approval
Every refund must be approved by a support specialist before it is issued.
